Backups

A backup is one file with all your secrets and contexts, encrypted with a backup password you choose. It opens without the storii server or your account, in the extension, the apps or the CLI. Keep the file somewhere safe and the password somewhere else.

storii is locked. Unlock it, then come back to this tab.

Restore a backup

Adds the backup’s items that aren’t in storii, exactly as they were backed up. Items storii already has are left as they are.
